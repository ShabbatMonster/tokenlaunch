DEVELOPER DETAILS 

    

  


  
A pump.fun coin stops trading the instant its curve fills, and stays dead until somebody
    calls migrate_v2 to drain the curve into a PumpSwap pool. pump's own bot normally does that
    within seconds — this is for when it does not. The call is permissionless: the coin's creator has no
    special standing, and whoever sends it collects no LP, because the liquidity is minted to the pool
    authority and burnt. The migration fee comes out of the curve's own balance, so all you pay is the
    transaction. Nothing is offered here until it simulates against the live program.
