Arc is Circle's L1, where USDC is the gas token. Its main DEX is a Uniswap V3 deployment that is not in any registry yet, so everything here was read off live trades. Tokens trade against USDC through the router at , and pools are found by asking the factory for each fee tier rather than by scanning logs.

Two things differ from stock Uniswap. There is no wrapped gas token: a buy spends USDC as an ordinary ERC-20, so the first trade of each token needs a one-time approval. And the router takes its swap arguments flat rather than as a struct. Keep a little USDC for gas even when selling.

The number shown before you swap comes from the pool's own price math and is exact while a trade stays inside one liquidity range. The minimum you actually accept is not taken from it: the exact swap is simulated first and your slippage is applied to that result, so a trade that crosses a range can never fill worse than you agreed to.
